FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does CairnGPS work without an internet connection?

Yes. Your phone’s GPS receives the satellites directly, with no mobile network or Wi-Fi. CairnGPS needs no connection for your position, the satellites or track recording: everything works offline, in the mountains as well as in airplane mode.

How can I see the GPS satellites my phone receives?

The Satellites tab shows every satellite on a sky plot by azimuth and elevation, lists its signal strength, and places the constellation above you on a 3D globe. You see how many satellites are in view and how many are used to compute your position.

Which satellite constellations are supported?

GPS (United States), GLONASS (Russia), Galileo (Europe), BeiDou (China), QZSS (Japan) and IRNSS/NavIC (India), depending on what your phone’s GNSS chip receives.

Why is the altitude accurate?

GPS measures a height above an ellipsoid, which can differ by several tens of metres from the altitude on maps. CairnGPS corrects it with the EGM96 geoid model to show the altitude above sea level.

Does track recording continue with the screen off?

Yes. Once a session is started you can pocket your phone: recording continues in the background and a persistent notification shows it. It never starts on its own, only when you start it.

Does CairnGPS replace a mapping app?

No: it shows no base map and does no routing. CairnGPS is a GNSS tool and logbook that complements your map app; GPX export lets you open your tracks in any software.

Do I need Google services to use CairnGPS?

No. CairnGPS uses neither Google Play Services nor Firebase, so it also works on phones without Google services. It is available on Google Play, on F-Droid and as an APK on GitHub.

Is CairnGPS free?

Yes: free, with no ads or in-app purchases, and open source under the AGPL-3.0 licence. It runs on Android 8.0 and later.

Privacy

Your tracks stay on your phone.

CairnGPS reads raw GPS through the Android APIs, with no Google Play Services, no Firebase and no tracking library. No account, no connection required. Read the privacy policy.
